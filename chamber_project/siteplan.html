Oaxaca de Juárez Chamber of Commerce

Website Site Plan

Name

Oaxaca de Juárez Chamber of Commerce

The website will represent the local business community of Oaxaca de Juárez and provide useful information for businesses, residents, and visitors.

Purpose

The Oaxaca de Juárez Chamber of Commerce website will serve as a resource for local businesses, entrepreneurs, residents, and visitors. The website will promote local businesses, provide information about community events and resources, and encourage networking within the business community. It will also help people discover businesses and services in Oaxaca de Juárez and learn how they can become members of the Chamber.

Target Market

The target market includes small business owners, businessman, local residents, visitors, and organizations in Oaxaca de Juárez. The website will especially serve people who want to discover local businesses, connect with other business owners, attend community events, or learn about Chamber membership.

Goals

  • Increase the visibility of local businesses.
  • Help businesses connect with the community.
  • Encourage entrepreneurs to participate in the Chamber.
  • Provide information about local events and resources.
  • Encourage people to support local businesses.

Scenarios

  • A local business owner wants to join the Chamber and needs information about membership benefits and requirements.
  • A resident wants to find a local restaurant, store, or service and uses the business directory.
  • An entrepreneur wants to connect with other business owners and find local networking opportunities.
  • A visitor wants to discover local businesses and interesting places in Oaxaca de Juárez.
  • A business owner wants to promote their business and learns about Chamber membership opportunities.

SEO Plan

  • Use keywords such as "Oaxaca de Juárez businesses" and "Oaxaca business directory."
  • Create unique meta descriptions for each page.
  • Use descriptive titles and headings.
  • Add meaningful alt text to images.
  • Use semantic HTML and accessible navigation.
  • Encourage local businesses to link to the Chamber website.
  • Use Google Analytics to monitor website traffic.
