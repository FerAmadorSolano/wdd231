Site Plan

Site Name

Xalapa Route

"Xalapa Route" is a digital guide focused on discovering places, activities, and experiences in Xalapa, Veracruz, and its surrounding areas.

The name represents the main purpose of the site: helping visitors and local residents discover different places and create their own routes around Xalapa.

Tagline: Discover what's nearby. Live differently.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Ruta Xalapa is to provide a digital guide for discovering places and experiences in Xalapa and nearby towns.

The website will provide information about nature, culture, coffee, gastronomy, nearby towns, and local experiences.

Users will be able to explore destinations, filter them by category, view detailed information about each place, and save their favorite destinations.

The site will also include a form that allows users to plan a visit based on the type of experience they are looking for.

Target Audience

  • People who live in Xalapa and want to discover new places.
  • Visitors traveling to Xalapa.
  • People looking for activities for a day trip.
  • People interested in nature, culture, coffee, and local gastronomy.

Scenarios

Scenario 1

What natural places can I visit near Xalapa for a day trip?

Scenario 2

What can I do in Xalapa if I want to learn more about the culture of the region?

Scenario 3

What places can I visit if I want to take a weekend trip near Xalapa?

Scenario 4

Where can I find places to enjoy coffee and local gastronomy?
